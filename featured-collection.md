# Featured collection

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (19).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Featured Collection section is designed to showcase multiple product collections in one compact, interactive area. Instead of scrolling through separate sections, customers can easily switch between collections using tabs—making it faster and more engaging to explore your products. Each tab highlights a specific collection, such as new arrivals, best sellers, seasonal picks, or curated categories.

**Configure your Featured Collection** **section:**

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (18).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Add a **Caption** to communicate the key message alongside the visual content

Enter the **Heading** text to display inside the section

Use the **Heading size** options to set the section’s heading text, 3 styles: Small, Medium, and Large, to display heading tabs.

**Columns**: number of columns to display, upper 5 columns

**Maximum items to show**: The maximum number of products per tab.

Show **Dots** to click next/prev product

Enable **Loop** posts in the section

Use **Auto-rotate slides** to the next product

Set **Change slides every (seconds)** time when active **Auto-rotate slides**

**Configure your Featured Collection Tabs** **block:**

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (20).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Add a **Heading** to show the custom collection’s name. Leave it blank if you want to display that collection’s name

The product will be displayed according to the **Collection** you select.
