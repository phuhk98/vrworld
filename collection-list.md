# Collection List

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (14).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

A collection list builds trust with your prospective customers by helping them see exactly what they’ll receive once they buy from you. It’s about creating collections one at a time, each designed to be business-effective. Show images of collections or shop moments to attract customers attractively.
