# How to enable social icons in the footer

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (43).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

First, you need to enter your social media links in the **Theme settings -> Social media**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (44).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Then, you need to enable the social icons in the footer **Social media** block.
