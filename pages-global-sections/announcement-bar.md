# Announcement bar

The Announcement Bar is a powerful way to instantly connect with your visitors by displaying important updates, promotions, or brand messages at the very top of your store. Whether it’s a limited-time sale, free shipping offer, new product launch, or an urgent notice, this section ensures your message stands out and reaches every visitor as soon as they land on your site.

**SET UP AN ANNOUNCEMENT BAR SECTION**

From the left side menu, select the **Announcement bar.**

Customize the section by utilizing the settings on the right side of the theme editor:

The block can add any promotional text. The text blocks will auto-set the scrolling text to move from Right to left. These blocks have general settings on the right side, like color, padding…
