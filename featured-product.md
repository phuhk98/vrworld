# Featured product

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (21).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Featured Product section is perfect for homepage promotions, campaign landing pages, or special seasonal highlights. By focusing customer attention on a single key item, it reduces distractions and encourages quicker purchasing decisions.

**Configure your Featured product** **section:**

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (22).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Use the **Color scheme** picker to select a color scheme for the section.

Add a **Product** to show.

Custom Image width from 30% to 70%.

With product information, dynamically arrange the **Horizontal** and **Vertical** positions.

Add blocks as Heading, Price, Variant picker, Button, Share… to display product information.
