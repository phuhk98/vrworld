# Multicolumn

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (28).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Multicolumn section (or feature blocks) allows you to display key business offerings, special services, or enterprise solutions side-by-side using high-impact visual cards. In your store, this is used to feature specific services like "Start Your VR Arcade" and "Customized XR Solutions", directing different target audiences (e.g., business owners vs. enterprise clients) to dedicated pages.

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (29).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Step-by-Step Customization Guide

Click on an individual Column block (e.g., _Column - Start Your VR Arcade_) under the main section in the left sidebar to customize its specific content in the right settings panel.

#### 1. Image Settings

* Image: Click on the thumbnail to upload or select a background image for the card block. High-resolution imagery representing the service works best here.

#### 2. Text Content Settings

* Heading: Enter the main title of the block in the text field (e.g., _"Start Your VR Arcade"_). You can apply formatting like Bold, _Italic_, or insert links.
* Description: Add supporting text using the rich text editor to explain the offer in detail (e.g., _"Exclusive VR arcade bundles, expert advice, and dedicated support for launching your VR arcade business."_).

#### 3. Call-to-Action (Button) Settings

* Button label: Specify the button text (e.g., _"LEARN MORE"_ or _"Explore Solutions"_).
* Button link: Paste or search for the destination page/URL where users will be redirected upon clicking the button.

#### 4. Block Management

* Remove block: Click the Remove block option (with the trash icon) at the bottom of the sidebar if you wish to delete this specific column card from the layout.
