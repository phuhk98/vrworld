# Testimonial

The Testimonial section helps build trust and credibility by showcasing authentic customer feedback, reviews, or quotes. It allows you to highlight positive experiences from real shoppers, brand ambassadors, or industry experts, giving new visitors the confidence to purchase from your store.

**Configure a block for your Scrolling promotion section**

Each block has some options: Text content, rating, and customer information, a product card to embellish your section and make it stand out more on your page.
