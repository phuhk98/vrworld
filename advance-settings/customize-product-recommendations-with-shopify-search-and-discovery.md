# Customize product recommendations with Shopify Search & Discovery

Certain themes display product recommendations on product pages to help customers discover related and complementary products. You can customize these recommendations using the Shopify Search & Discovery app.

**Create product recommendations**

Customize product recommendations that display on your product page.

#### Steps: <a href="#steps" id="steps"></a>

From your Shopify admin, go to **Apps** > [**Search & Discovery**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/search-and-discovery).

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (70).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Click **Product recommendations**, select a product that you want to customize product recommendations for.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (72).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Select up to 10 **Complementary products**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (73).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Select up to 10 **Related products** to recommend. Set the product to display only the custom recommendations or to display both custom recommendations and automatically generated recommendations.

Click **Save**.

**Customizing the appearance of complementary products**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (74).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Heading**: The title of the block.

**Maximum products to show**: Select the maximum number of complementary products to display. Select a value between 1 and 10.

**Padding bottom**: Add bottom padding to the block below. Select a value between 0px and 100px.
