# Video with text

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (25).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The VR Video with Text section combines rich visual storytelling with compelling text and call-to-action (CTA) elements. It is designed to engage users by showcasing your VR products, accessories, or experiences in dynamic video format—helping boost customer interest and conversions far better than static images alone.

### Step-by-Step Customization Guide

Use the settings in the right sidebar panel of the Theme Editor to configure the section layout, video source, text content, and button settings.

#### 1. General & Layout Settings

* Color scheme: Choose a preset color palette to adjust the background and text color styling for this section.
* Make section full width: Enable this toggle if you want the video background/container to stretch across the entire screen width without side margins.
* Height: Set the height scale of the section (e.g., _Small_, _Medium_, _Large_).
* Desktop video placement: Select how the video is displayed relative to the text content on desktop devices (e.g., _Video full_ overlay or side-by-side).

#### 2. Video Settings

* Video source: Choose whether to load your video from an external platform (_Vimeo or YouTube_) or directly via Shopify hosting.
* Vimeo or Youtube video URL: Paste the URL link of your video (e.g., `[https://www.youtube.com/](https://www.youtube.com/)...`).
* Shopify hosted video: Click Select to upload or pick an MP4 video file stored directly in your Shopify files.
* Cover image: Upload a custom preview image (`video-poster.jpg`) that displays before the video plays or acts as a fallback thumbnail.

#### 3. Content Settings

* Subheading: Add a smaller secondary header above the main title to provide extra context.
* Heading: Input your main headline (e.g., _"See Your VR Accessories in Action Immerse Yourself in the Experience"_). You can format text using Bold, _Italic_, or add links.
* Heading size: Adjust the typography size using the dropdown menu (e.g., _Small_, _Medium_, _Large_).
* Description: Add detailed rich-text body content using bullets, lists, or custom formatting beneath the heading.

#### 4. Button & Alignment Settings

* Button label: Enter the text displayed inside the Call-to-Action button (e.g., _"Learn More"_).
* Link: Insert the target URL or search destination where users will be redirected when clicking the button.
* Alignment (Desktop & Mobile): Choose how the text and button are aligned (_Left_, _Center_, or _Right_) independently for desktop and mobile views.
* Section padding: Fine-tune top and bottom spacing to separate this section cleanly from adjacent content blocks.
