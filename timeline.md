# Timeline

The Timeline section is a storytelling tool that enables you to showcase your brand’s journey, product evolution, or key milestones in a clear and engaging format. Designed to display events in chronological order, the timeline helps customers understand your history, growth, and achievements at a glance.

**Configure your Timeline section**

Add a **Caption** to communicate the key message alongside the visual content

Enter the **Heading** text to display inside the section

Use the **Heading size** options to set the section’s heading text&#x20;

**Configure a block for your Timeline section**



An **Image** to describe more content.

In the **Heading** text box, enter text to display inside each block.

Add a **Year** to show the timeline of your journey.

In the **Description** box, enter text to display inside the section. Format the text and add links, using the Text editor pane.
